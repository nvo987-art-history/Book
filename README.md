# NVO987 Bookstore

**Independent Publishing | Nicholas Van-Orton**

Website: https://books.nvo987.us/

NVO987 Bookstore is the independent publishing platform of
Nicholas Van-Orton.

It presents research-based books, scholarly publications,
documentation, and cultural works focused on art history,
visual culture, history, and cultural memory.

## About

NVO987 is a multidisciplinary artistic and cultural initiative
exploring the intersections of visual culture and art history.

The publishing initiative provides a platform for publications
that contribute to the documentation, interpretation, and
preservation of cultural and historical knowledge.

Its publications may include:

- Art-historical research
- Monographic studies
- Cultural research
- Historical documentation
- Visual culture studies
- Interdisciplinary publications
- Independent authorial works
