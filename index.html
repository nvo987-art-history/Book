About NVO987

Publishing as an act of cultural preservation.

NVO987 is an independent publishing initiative curated by Nicholas Van-Orton. Its purpose is to create considered physical editions for cultural, artistic, and research-driven work that deserves to exist beyond the speed and disposability of the digital feed.

Each publication is conceived as an object of inquiry: a meeting point between research, authorship, visual culture, and material form. The catalogue is intentionally selective, allowing each volume to receive the attention, context, and permanence that serious work merits.
