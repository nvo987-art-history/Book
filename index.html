The Publisher

Research, culture, and artistic inquiry — published with intention.

NVO987 is an independent publishing platform curated by Nicholas Van-Orton, dedicated to bringing cultural, artistic, intellectual, and research-driven works into considered physical form. Each publication is approached as both an archive and an object: a durable record of ideas, investigations, visual language, and human curiosity. The mission is simple — preserve meaningful research, encourage deeper cultural conversation, and create books worthy of being discovered, studied, and kept.
