The Library

Books made for deeper looking.

NVO987 is a boutique publishing house devoted to books that reward attention. We publish cultural, artistic, and research works that examine the ideas, histories, images, and human experiences shaping our world.

Each publication is approached as an intellectual and material object: carefully considered, deliberately edited, and designed to remain meaningful beyond the moment of publication.

The aim is not simply to add more books to the world, but to create works with enough depth, clarity, and character to become part of an enduring cultural conversation.

Editorial Philosophy

Curiosity is the beginning.
Depth is the destination.

NVO987 brings together research, cultural inquiry, artistic practice, and considered publishing to create books with lasting intellectual value.

The Publisher

Nicholas Van-Orton.

I created NVO987 from a conviction that publishing can be more than the production of books. It can be an act of cultural preservation, intellectual exploration, and meaningful communication.

My vision is to develop publications that investigate subjects with patience and seriousness while remaining accessible, visually compelling, and deeply human. I am interested in the territory where research meets culture, where art meets history, and where individual observation opens onto larger questions.

NVO987 is therefore intentionally selective. The goal is to publish works that have something substantial to say—and to give those works the editorial attention and visual presence they deserve.
