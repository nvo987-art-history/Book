The Publisher

Research with cultural depth.

NVO987 is an independent publishing imprint dedicated to thoughtful cultural, artistic, and research-driven works. Each publication is approached as an enduring object: rigorous in its ideas, deliberate in its presentation, and created for readers who value depth over noise.

Curated by Nicholas Van-Orton, the catalogue explores intersections between culture, visual expression, history, identity, and independent inquiry. The mission is simple: preserve meaningful research, give distinctive ideas a considered form, and place them in the hands of curious readers.

Nicholas Van-Orton
