The Publisher

Independent publishing with a long-term cultural perspective.

NVO987 is an independent publishing imprint established and directed by Nicholas Van-Orton.

The publishing programme is concerned with art history, cultural research and the study of visual culture, with particular attention to projects grounded in original research and sustained scholarly inquiry.

NVO987 treats the book as a durable form of cultural documentation. Publications are developed to preserve research, establish a clear editorial record and make substantial work accessible beyond the moment of its initial publication.

The catalogue is intentionally selective. Each title is regarded as an individual intellectual and editorial project rather than as part of a high-volume publishing programme.

Color as a Generative Principle in the Work of Sonia Delaunay

An art-historical research publication by Nicholas Van-Orton examining the role of colour and its generative function in the work of Sonia Delaunay.

The study considers colour as an active structural principle within Delaunay's artistic work and examines its relationship to the development of her visual language.

Research & Culture

Publication as a form of cultural preservation.

NVO987 connects independent research with the permanence and accessibility of the book.

The imprint publishes works that document research, artistic practices and cultural subjects with lasting scholarly and historical relevance.

Particular emphasis is placed on art history, cultural history and visual culture, while the publishing programme remains open to research projects that cross conventional disciplinary boundaries.

Publications may appear in different languages and formats according to the requirements of each project. Every edition maintains its own bibliographic identity and publication record.
