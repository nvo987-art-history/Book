The Publishing House

Books for curious minds.

NVO987 is an independent publishing identity dedicated to cultural, artistic, and research-led books. Its purpose is simple: to give meaningful ideas the space, attention, and physical form they deserve.

Curated by Nicholas Van-Orton, the catalogue is conceived around subjects that reward close observation — from cultural histories and artistic practices to archival discoveries, critical inquiry, and contemporary research. Each publication is approached as both an intellectual object and a lasting cultural artifact.

NVO987 values clarity over noise, substance over volume, and distinctive perspectives over convention. The result is a publishing practice designed for readers, researchers, collectors, and anyone drawn to ideas with depth and character.
