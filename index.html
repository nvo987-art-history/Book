The Publisher

Independent publishing with a long-term cultural perspective.

NVO987 is an independent publishing imprint established by Nicholas Van-Orton.

The imprint publishes books arising from research in art history, cultural studies and related fields of scholarly inquiry.

NVO987 is conceived as a long-term publishing programme rather than a limited catalogue. Individual publications are developed as independent works while forming part of a growing research and cultural archive.

The programme is deliberately selective, placing emphasis on original scholarship, responsible documentation and the preservation of research in durable print and digital editions.

The Catalogue

Research preserved through publication.

Future NVO987 publications will continue to address art history, cultural research, visual culture and related fields of scholarly inquiry.

Each publication will be presented as an individual research work within the broader NVO987 publishing programme.

Research

A publishing programme built around original research.

NVO987 publishes research in which historical, cultural and visual questions are examined through sustained scholarly investigation.

The imprint supports publications that contribute to the documentation, interpretation and continued accessibility of significant cultural research.

Publications may appear in different languages and editions according to the nature and intended readership of each research work.
