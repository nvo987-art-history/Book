The Publishing House

Research with a point of view.

NVO987 is an independent publishing imprint dedicated to cultural, artistic, and intellectual research. We create carefully considered publications that connect visual culture, history, ideas, and contemporary creative practice.

Every title is approached as an object of lasting value: researched with rigor, edited with intention, and presented with the clarity and visual language it deserves.

Our mission is simple: preserve meaningful research, amplify distinctive perspectives, and give curious readers books worth returning to.
